# Email Copy Rules

Guidelines for writing effective, professional outreach emails with ClientPilot AI.

## Subject Line

- Keep under 60 characters for best open rates
- Be specific, not generic: "Quick question about your website" not "Hello"
- Do not use ALL CAPS
- Do not use exclamation marks in subject
- Do not use spam trigger words (free, guarantee, limited time)

## Body

### Do

- Start with a polite greeting using the recipient's name
- Introduce yourself and your business in the first paragraph
- Reference something specific about their business (website, recent post, service)
- State one clear value proposition
- End with one specific call to action

### Do Not

- Do not make fake claims or guarantees
- Do not use manipulation tactics (urgency, scarcity, false exclusivity)
- Do not write more than 150-180 words for initial emails
- Do not use more than 2 exclamation marks
- Do not include attachments or images in initial outreach

## Personalization

- Mention their company name
- Reference their industry or location
- Note something specific from their website
- Do not use template placeholders like [Name] or [Company]
- If you cannot personalize, do not send

## Call to Action

- One clear CTA per email
- Make it easy to say yes: "Would you be open to a 15-minute call?"
- Avoid hard closes: "Sign up now" or "Buy today"
- End with a question to encourage reply

## Unsubscribe

- Every email must include an unsubscribe link
- The unsubscribe link is added automatically by ClientPilot AI
- Do not remove the unsubscribe line
- Process unsubscribe requests promptly

## Follow-ups

- Wait at least 2 days before first follow-up
- Keep follow-ups shorter than the initial email
- Reference your previous email politely
- Do not follow up more than 3 times without a reply
- Stop immediately if they say not interested

## Tone

- Professional but conversational
- Helpful, not salesy
- Polite, not pushy
- Respectful of their time

## Before Sending

- Read the draft out loud
- Check for typos
- Verify the company name is correct
- Confirm the personalization line is accurate
- Check quality score in the editor
